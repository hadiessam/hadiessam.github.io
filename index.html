Case study 02

Performance recovery during platform volatility

IRA Academy · 15 Oct – 30 Nov 2025 · AI and Cybersecurity Strategies diploma
TL 130.62Peak CPL during volatility
TL 50.79CPL after recovery
61%CPL reduction
1.71%CTR after relaunch

The problem

  • Mid-flight, cost per lead jumped from TL 48.82 to TL 130.62, a 168% increase.
  • No changes had been made to targeting, creative or bidding. The shift was platform-side.
  • Standard levers such as budget shifts and audience tweaks were not recovering delivery.

What I did

  • Isolated the variable: confirmed no internal change, so treated it as delivery instability.
  • Paused the affected campaign instead of continuing to spend into rising costs.
  • Duplicated the campaign to reset the learning phase and relaunched with optimized settings.
  • Monitored early delivery metrics closely and reallocated budget once CPL stabilized.

Outcome

  • Recovered cost per lead to TL 50.79, a 61% reduction from the peak.
  • The relaunched campaign delivered 122 leads at the recovered CPL with a 1.71% CTR.
  • Avoided writing off the campaign. Pausing and relaunching preserved the budget instead of burning it on a degraded delivery curve.
Why this one matters: anyone can scale a campaign that is already working. This is what happens when the platform moves underneath you and the numbers stop making sense. The job is to diagnose before reacting.

How I work

A repeatable loop, not one-off campaigns.

Every program follows the same operating sequence. The discipline is in running it consistently across 27 concurrent offers.

01

Objective and KPI definition

Set the target before spending: cost per lead ceiling, volume expectation, and the quality bar a lead has to clear to count.

02

Audience architecture

Build in layers: core interest targeting, lookalike expansion from existing converters, and retargeting pools for form openers and engaged users.

03

Creative testing system

Produce multiple variants per concept and test systematically. Kill underperformers early; put budget behind what the data backs.

04

Funnel friction reduction

Optimize Instant Form fields and the landing experience to lift completion rate without lowering lead intent.

05

Daily performance monitoring

Track cost per lead, CTR and delivery health daily. Volatility gets caught in the first 48 hours, not at the end of the month.

06

Optimization and scaling

Reallocate budget toward the ad sets holding efficiency, and scale only while the cost curve stays flat.
